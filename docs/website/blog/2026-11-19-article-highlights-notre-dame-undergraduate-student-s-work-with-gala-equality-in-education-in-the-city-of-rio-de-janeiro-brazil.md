Notre Dame undergraduate student Ana Beatriz D Maya Ferreira was featured in an article from the Keough School of Global Affairs at Notre Dame for her work exploring educational deserts in relation to geography in her home country of Brazil. As a student in Prof. Rebecca Hardin's 6-week innovation lab, Bia decided to create a new Gala module as her final project. She created a learning module: *Equality in education in the city of Rio de Janeiro*, Brazil, complete with original maps and research. After the lab concluded, she continued working with Rebecca and the Gala team to develop her module and advance her research.
